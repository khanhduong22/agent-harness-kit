Checkout request flow

hud-monochrome style · layered left-to-right · six components

Reading the motion: marching dashes mean traffic is flowing on that connector, a moving dot is one in-flight request, the glowing node is actively processing, and the filling bar is queue depth. Dashed links are asynchronous; the amber long-short dash is the error path. With reduced motion enabled every animation renders its end state, so nothing here depends on animation to be understood.
